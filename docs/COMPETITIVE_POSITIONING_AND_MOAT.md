# Atlas Competitive Positioning and Moat

This is a strategy reference, not a claim about competitors' private systems.

## Position

Atlas should compete on controlled automation rather than unverified bot counts:

AI proposes. Evidence validates. Risk controls decide. Atlas executes only when the required gates pass, and material actions are auditable and reconciled.

## Strengths to build around

- evidence-first research and validation;
- paper-first onboarding;
- customer ledger and reconciliation controls;
- risk gates and kill-switch controls;
- explainable strategy and execution state;
- explicit separation between research capability and execution authority.

## Known product gaps

- Some plan labels still describe capabilities that require further entitlement/E2E proof.
- Alerts can be configuration-only without a proven evaluator/notification worker.
- Some automation paths are paper-oriented.
- Binance triangular-arbitrage live execution remains disabled pending unified multi-leg lifecycle/recovery.
- API access and priority support require real service commitments, not plan strings.
- Billing requires staging lifecycle and reconciliation evidence.
- Competitor pricing/features must be checked against official sources before publication.

## Marketing evidence rule

Do not publish a competitor price, exchange-support claim, regulatory claim, or product weakness without an official source and date.

Do not publish an Atlas paid benefit as fully delivered until its entitlement, implementation, regression, and required E2E evidence agree.

## Defensible differentiation

Show customers evidence rather than promises:

backtest -> out-of-sample -> walk-forward where applicable -> paper observation -> costs -> drawdown -> risk status -> approval -> execution/reconciliation status.
