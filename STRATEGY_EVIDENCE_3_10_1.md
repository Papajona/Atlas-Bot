# Strategy Evidence and Promotion Policy

Atlas does not label any strategy a guaranteed or universally winning strategy.

Evidence incorporated into the research gate:
- Time-series momentum/trend following has documented long-run, cross-asset evidence (Moskowitz/Ooi/Pedersen; Hurst/Ooi/Pedersen).
- Momentum can experience severe crashes; risk management/volatility management is therefore a first-class control.
- Backtests must include costs, slippage, out-of-sample/walk-forward testing and forward paper/shadow validation.
- Stop-loss clustering, drawdown and daily-loss halts are treated as protective overlays, not alpha.

Promotion requires positive out-of-sample evidence, acceptable drawdown, sufficient observations, cost sensitivity, and paper/shadow consistency. Historical results never guarantee future returns.
